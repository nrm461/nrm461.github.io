CARUSO
@baron_films

Here is our short film CARUSO which you can see here or on your bigger screen on @nowness

 

Set in Noto, Sicily, CARUSO follows an aspiring motocross rider as he navigates the pressures of youth. The short blurs the lines between documentary and narrative by using non-actors and placing them in familiar scenarios. CARUSO highlights the Sicilian cultural pillars of religion and family, which stand strong even in our ever changing world.

 

This was made for next to nothing with the hard work and generosity of family and friends across the globe. Making these types of projects brings me back to the pure joy of filmmaking.
